Sigil is a location-based social app inspired by the message system in FromSoftware's Soulsborne games. Users compose short messages ("inscriptions") from predefined word banks, optionally attach a photo, and pin them to real-world GPS coordinates. Nearby users can discover these inscriptions on a map or in a nearby feed and "appraise" the ones that resonate with them.
