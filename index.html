How it works

✦

Inscribe

Compose a message from curated word banks, in the tradition of the notes wanderers leave for one another in FromSoftware's action RPGs. Attach a photo and pin it to a real place, either as an exact pin or somewhere within a 500 m area.

◎

Discover

Browse inscriptions on a live map or in the nearby feed as you move through the world. Opt in to a quiet notification when you pass one you haven't seen. Those alerts are worked out on your phone, not on our servers.

⚜

Appraise

One appraisal per inscription, and no downvotes. Your Legacy tracks the inscriptions you've placed and the appraisals they've earned.

Sigil+

Bear the mark of Sigil+, and set your legacy apart

  • ✦Inscribe without limit, with no hourly cap
  • ✦Choose your pin's color, shape, or emoji upon the map
  • ✦Give your inscriptions a color and frame all their own
  • ✦Wear a bordered avatar upon your Legacy
  • ✦Feature a single inscription above the rest

7-day free trial, then $1.99 USD a month or $16.99 USD a year
